Questions

Good to know.

How do I choose the right offer?

Start with a discovery conversation. If you know something is off but not what, the Operations Clarity Audit is a strong first step. If you have one specific problem, the Strategy Intensive fits. If you want ongoing leadership support, the Fractional CSOO Partnership is the long-term option.

Do you work remotely or in person?

Both. I work with clients across Dallas-Fort Worth in person and with clients nationwide remotely.

Do you work with nonprofits?

Yes. I support nonprofits and purpose-driven organizations with strategic planning, organizational assessments, executive search support, and board-ready deliverables.

What happens after the discovery call?

I'll follow up with a recommendation on which offer fits best, or tell you honestly if I'm not the right fit. You decide the next step, and there is no pressure.
