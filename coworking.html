For Coworking Owners and Operators

Coworking is where I work every day.

I run business operations for a Dallas coworking space, day to day. I'm not advising from a distance. I handle the member emails, the room bookings, the partnership agreements, and the invoices. That experience goes into every engagement with a coworking owner or operator, and it's why I can help you improve operational efficiency and community engagement at the same time.

01

Member Experience and Community Building

Members stay when they feel they belong. I manage member relations daily and design experiences that bring people together, like a National Coworking Day scavenger hunt that got members meeting each other. I help you build a community people want to be part of.

02

Partnerships, Events, and Programming

I build venue and community partnerships with nonprofits and business associations, write the MoUs that formalize them, and manage room and event bookings. I also run local vendor showcases that put neighborhood businesses in front of your members.

03

Operations, Facilities, and A/R

The work behind the scenes keeps a space running: facilities coordination, billing, and recovering past-due payments. My space shares a building with residential lofts, so I know how to host lively events while respecting the neighbors upstairs, from the right room and end time to clear noise terms in every agreement.

04

Team, Front Desk, and Succession

I lead a front-desk and member-experience team, and I plan for the day a key person moves on. Right now I'm designing a university internship pilot to build a pipeline for that role, so your member experience doesn't depend on one person.
