About

The person behind the work.

A fractional COO and operations executive who builds systems, teams, and communities that scale without losing stability. Also a mom, a music lover, and a communications nerd, and all of it shows up in how I work.

My Story

I lead through relationships and trust.

I started my career in a classroom. I spent 14 years in public education, and I learned there that no program, schedule, or system works unless the people inside it feel seen and supported. That belief has shaped every role I've held since.

As an educator I led a district college-and-career-readiness program with results I'm still proud of. Building the structure around those students was operations work, even before I called it that. When I moved into executive leadership, I kept the same priorities: clear systems, honest communication, and people who have what they need to do their best work.

100%of program students graduated and were accepted to college
81%earned scholarships
$1M+in scholarship awards

I'm a communications nerd at heart (my BA is in Communication Studies, and I'm now earning an MA in the same field). I believe most operational problems are communication problems in disguise, and most of them get solved by listening first. I care about equity and fairness, and about giving people access to the resources they need to thrive.

Today I bring 15+ years of executive leadership, operations, and consulting to founders, nonprofits, school districts, and community spaces.
